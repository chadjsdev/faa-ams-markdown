## General Tasks

The contractor must provide quality technical, engineering, analytical, and planning support to achieve the requirements of this SOW. # Specific Tasks

## System Integration

The contractor must provide support to [*name of organization*] for the integration of new or modified [*name of system(s)*] systems into the NAS.

The contractor must perform the following activities:

* Develop system specifications to ensure the system will meet or exceed the requirements of the NAS
* Monitor the design and development of interfaces between new [*name of system(s)*] systems, [*name of system(s)*], ... and *[name of system(s)*] systems to ensure system compatibility with the NAS
* Ensure Interface Design Documents and Interface Control Documents are complete and accurately reflect system interface behavior
* Review and provide recommendations on any changes, improvements or deletions that are to be made to physical, functional, and other required multi-level interfaces among [*name of system(s)*] products at the sub-system and system level
* Monitor sub-system and/or system testing, as required
* Monitor the installation, integration and validation of the system to ensure that the system behaves as documented when integrated into the NAS, as required
* Identify System Integration issues, provide resolutions and track to closure

The contractor must attend Design Reviews, Technical Interchange Meetings, and working groups in the area of System Integration.

## Facility Engineering

The contractor must provide support to [*name of organization*] in facility and site planning, design and implementation for all projects within [*name of organization*]. The contractor must identify and analyze any issues that may impact each facility, and recommend solutions.

The contractor must perform the following:

* Coordinate with the facility and other FAA organizations (including ACB, ARN, ASD, ANI, AFZ and AOS) to define the proper location for the system
* Preparing, coordinating and tracking NAS Change Proposals (NCP) and/or Electronic Equipment Modifications (EEM) needed to place the system at the facility
* Track all facility action items to closure
* Maintain facility schedules

The contractor must attend Design Reviews, Technical Interchange Meetings, and working groups in the area of System Integration.

## System Deployment

The contractor must support the coordination necessary to deploy a new system at an FAA facility. The contractor must perform the following:

* Prepare, schedule and support pre-briefings, site coordination telecoms, Pre-Site Surveys, Site Surveys, training needs assessments and other coordination activities required to successfully deploy a system into an operational environment
* Track and maintain the status of any action items that occur during the conduct of any implementation activities
* Coordinate with those regions, sectors and centers affected by a [*type of system*] system deployment
* Prepare and maintain an Implementation Plan for all projects within [*name of organization*] describing the activities of each implementation, detailed schedules and milestones necessary to track the progress of each implementation
* Review and provide comments to all Contract Data Requirements List (CDRL) item documents in the area of system implementation
* Support the activities for the decommissioning and removal of equipment from each site, and disposal of the equipment in accordance with FAA disposal procedures
* The contractor must participate in design reviews and site inspections, and participate in Implementation working groups.

## Environmental/Site Surveys

The contractor must provide environmental and natural resource management support to [*name of organization*] that will ensure compliance with the FAA policies, regulations and federal environment laws. The contractor must support [*name of organization*] in promoting environmentally sound implementation of programs and projects. The contractor must conduct environmental studies on a programmatic or site-specific basis and prepare and review environmental documents covered by the National Environmental Policy Act, the regulations of the Council on Environmental Quality, and FAA Order 1050.1D.

The contractor must perform the following activities as required for each program or project within [*name of organization*]:

* Prepare Environmental Impact Statements, Environmental Assessments, Categorical Exclusions, and Findings of No Significant Impact
* Perform Environmental Due Diligence Audits
* Support the development of environmental documentation, presentations, schedules, databases and spreadsheets
* Prepare and present training on specific aspects of environmental science
* Participate in FAA-wide working groups for the development of new or revised policy and procedures for compliance with environmental laws, regulations and/or Executive Orders
* Prepare for and conduct public meetings related to environmental issues
* Assist in leasing or purchasing sites and obtaining land-use approvals
* Obtain FAA approvals necessary to acquire or dispose of sites
* Participate in site survey planning, update survey reports and maintain library of reports
* Coordinate site location changes with approving agencies
* Perform special studies as required
* Provide program management and administrative support for implementation activities
* Respond to Freedom of Information Act requests
* Develop and test prototypes for animal deterrence

## Investment Analysis

The contractor must provide implementation engineering support for investment analysis activities (For more information go to http://fast.faa.gov, select Investment Analysis). The contractor must support the following activities:

* Perform Market Surveys for new requirements and capabilities and evaluate each capabilities against the NAS architecture for technical and cost feasibility
* Perform Alternatives Analysis of available technologies to answer a Mission Need
* Perform Investment Analyses of each alternative, including returns on investment, comparisons among each alternative and ability to integrate the capability into the NAS

The contractor must support the preparation of reports and other documentation required to assist in the development of Acquisition Program Baseline documents and Joint Resource Council briefings
