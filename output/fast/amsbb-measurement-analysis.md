# Measurement & Analysis

[AMS Policy Section 2.1.5](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.1.pdf#nameddest=policy2_1_5 "AMS Policy Section 2.1.5")

A management and control process applied throughout the lifecycle of an investment program to track status and warn of emerging problems before they become severe issues.

## What Must be Done

Collect, store, analyze, & report program data on 17 standard measures.

## Who Does It

Lines of business and staff offices institute measurement & analysis processes.

Service teams and program offices collect, analyze, and report program data.

## Who Approves

AFN Finance assists service teams and program offices with measurement and control reporting.
