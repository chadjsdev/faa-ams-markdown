# Human Factors

[AMS Policy Section 4.7](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy4.7.pdf "AMS Policy Section 4.7")

Human factors is incorporated into product design from the outset to optimize performance and capitalize on the
strengths of people and machines.

## What Must be Done

Address human factors throughout the lifecycle of FAA products and services (see Human Factors Job Aid in the Toolkit).

## Who Does It

Human factors resources are available in the ATO Program Management Organization, NextGen Organization, and ATO Office of Safety and Technical Services to work with service organizations and program offices.

## Who Approves

Document templates specify approval authorities.
