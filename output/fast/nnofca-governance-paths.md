# Governance Paths

[AMS Policy Section 2.8](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.8.pdf "AMS Policy Section 2.8")

The Program Office/Line of Business prepares the intake form presenting it to the Operations Governance Board (OGB) Secretariat at least 21 days prior to OGB meeting.
The Secretariat conducts a preliminary risk review based on the following criteria:

  

* Visibility (Internal and External)
* Time Sensitivity
* Cost Structure and Estimate
* Number and Location of Performance Sites
* Complexity of the Effort

Based on this review the Secretariat makes a Governance Path recommendation to the OGB.
The OGB then assigns one of the following governance paths to Mission Support Ops Funded investment initiatives at the readiness decision meeting or returns the initiative to the sponsoring line of business for additional work or terminates the initiative.

Governance Path A  is for initiatives posing low risk.

* The Secretariat makes a recommendation to the full OGB indicating Governance Path A
  is appropriate
* At the Readiness Decision the OGB can concur with this recommendation, and the program is approved
* The Program Office is required to complete the appropriate artifacts as directed by the OGB
* There will only be random spot reviews for those artifacts. Approval should take seven 7 days

Governance Path B  is for initiatives posing low to medium risk.

* In this path, the Secretariat monitors the Program Office progress toward completion of the required artifacts with a checklist
* At the Readiness Decision the OGB may approve the initiative then, pending completion of the checklist, or delay approval until the checklist and artifacts are verified to be complete
* The OGB Secretariat reviews all required artifacts to verify completion. Random spot reviews are conducted to assess and validate artifact contents

Governance Path C  is for initiatives posing high risk.

* Initiatives directed to follow this governance path will, at the Readiness Decision, be assigned an Acquisition Readiness Team (ART),
  which will review and verify all required artifacts. An item is only marked complete on the Secretariat checklist after having been approved by the ART
* When all artifacts are complete and verified, and a scaled business case is ready, the initiative will return to the OGB for the Business Case Decision
* If approved at the Business Case Decision,
  the ART and the Program office further refine the artifacts and complete the Final Scaled Business Case, which,
  after verification by the ART, is presented at the Investment Commitment Decision
  Approval at that decision point allows the program to enter the deployment phase. Final approval should take 60-90 days
