## [What Must be Done](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.6.pdf#nameddest=policy2_6_1 "What Must be Done")

Finalize and sustain planning; obtain the solution; verify operational readiness;
verify and validate key work products; plan for in-service management; prepare for
the in-service decision; and deploy the solution at all sites.

## [Who Does It](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.6.pdf#nameddest=policy2_6_3 "Who Does It")

Service organization or program office supported by key stakeholders, operating
service organization, ATO Safety & Technical Training, and AIO Information
Technology Program Office.

## [Who Approves](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.6.pdf#nameddest=policy2_6_4 "Who Approves")

Program manager makes day-to-day program decisions.

Vice President (ATO) or Director (other LOBs) of the implementing service
organization makes the production demonstration and production decisions. (if
applicable and not otherwise designated by the JRC)

## [Key Outputs and Products](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.6.pdf#nameddest=policy2_6_2 "Key Outputs and Products")

* [In-Service Decision](https://fast.faa.gov/NFFCA_In_Service_Decision.cfm "In-Service Decision")
* Operational capability deployed at each site
* [Integrated Logistics Support](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy.pdf#nameddest=policy4_3_3_4 "Integrated Logistics Support")
* Plan for In-Service management
* [Acquisition quarterly & other program reviews](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy1.2.pdf#nameddest=policy1_2_7 "Acquisition quarterly and other program reviews")
* [Independent Operational Assessment (designated programs)](https://fast.faa.gov/EMP_Independent_Operational_Assessment.cfm "Independent Operational Assessment (designated programs)")
