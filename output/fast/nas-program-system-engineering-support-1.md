# Scope

## General Tasks

The contractor must provide quality technical, engineering, analytical, and planning support to achieve the requirements of this SOW. The contractor must furnish and make available all personnel, supplies, equipment, materials, data, facilities, and services necessary to assist the *[name of organization*] in accomplishing its mission. As determined by the FAA, the contractor may be required to interface with system integration contractors, equipment manufacturers, airport personnel, various FAA and U.S. Government and Military organizations, and international organizations such as Eurocontrol and NAV Canada.

The contractor must provide support in the areas of [*name of system(s), i.e. communication, navigation, or surveillance*] systems research and development, design, development, test, production, operations, and architecture. As such, the contractor must possess demonstrable experience in and a detailed understanding of [*name of system(s), i.e. communication, navigation, or surveillance*] systems.

## Program Management

The contractor must provide a full range of program management support to [*name of organization*]. The contractor must provide support to all projects within [*name of organization*] in the development and maintenance of schedules, process flow charts, Memorandums of Understanding/Agreements, and plans necessary to monitor and control each project. This support must include:

* Coordinating with the appropriate FAA organizations, regions, sector offices, the FAA Logistics Center, the FAA Academy, or the William J. Hughes Technical Center
* Participating in team meetings, working groups, program reviews, senior level status reviews, and team building exercises
* Tracking and processing of actions and resolutions, NAS changes proposals, and program directive
* Tracking identified risks and documenting potential work-around and/or resolution
* Tracking existing program configurations, issues and status
* Providing support for the development of project memos, presentations, handouts, status charts, and position papers

## Human Factors

The contractor must provide systems engineering support in the following Human Factors activities for all systems in [*name of organization*], including:

* Identifying and defining system-specific human factors requirements
* Assessing human factors risks
* Providing technical solutions to mitigate human factors risks
* Advising on decisions related to human factors engineering
* Implementing Human Factors Plans and processes

The contractor must provide support to analyze human-computer interfaces, staffing and training, workload, procedures, documentation, communications, and other human-system interface issues.

## Concept Analysis

The contractor must provide support associated with identifying and analyzing candidate alternatives and concepts that address [*name of system(s)*] system issues, shortfalls, technological defects, end-of-service issues, and other issues. This support must include:

* Ensuring compatibility with NAS architecture
* Supporting Life Cycle Cost estimation and Cost-Benefits Analysis
* Performing risk assessments
* Evaluating supportability
* Evaluating operational or procedural impacts
* Evaluating environmental impacts
* Evaluating overall NAS schedule impacts.

## System Development/Integration

The contractor must support system development/integration activities for all systems in [*name of organization*]. The contractor must support the development of requirements, specifications, and requirements traceability. The contractor must review system documentation for the design, checkout, integration, and testing of [*name of system(s)*] systems at the factory, test sites, and field sites. The contractor must monitor the manufacturer's compliance with specifications. The contractor must perform technical analyses related to [*name of system(s)*] systems hardware engineering, system interfaces, software engineering, and software maintenance. The contractor must support the design and implementation of interfaces between new [*name of system(s)*] systems, [*name of system(s)*], and [*name of systems*] systems.

The contractor must perform technical evaluations and analyses of [*name of system(s)*] systems in the following areas:

* Logistics
* Safety
* Service Life Extension Planning
* Test Planning

The contractor must support technical interchange meetings and design reviews on [*name of system(s)*] system components, software, and interfaces. This must include reviewing design changes and ensuring that the system meets all requirements of the specification and referenced standards as well as engineering practices. The contractor must support working group meetings concerning software, interfaces, hardware and specialty engineering. The contractor must review upgrades and modifications of any Non-Developmental Items (NDI) and identify any issues or concerns, as well as present potential resolutions

The contractor must provide technical assistance in documenting system transition requirements, assist in the generation of transition plans, and in the actual transition of services.

## System Production

The contractor must support system production activities for all systems in [*name of organization*]. The contractor must perform the following activities:

* Review system documentation for the First Article testing of [*name of system(s)*] systems at the factory, and production testing at all test sites and field sites
* Monitor the manufacturer's compliance with production specifications.
* Observe First Article testing and production testing, as required.
* Perform technical analyses related to the production of *[name of system(s)*] systems, including problem management, hardware maintenance, system interfaces, and software maintenance.

## System Performance

The contractor must support system performance activities for all systems in [*name of organization*]. The contractor must support the following activities:

* Development of performance specifications for [*name of system(s)*] systems
* Monitoring the manufacturer's compliance with specifications
* Collecting, analyzing and evaluating system performance data for new and existing systems
* Reviewing modifications to [*name of system(s)*] systems and analyze their impact to system performance

The contractor must support technical interchange meetings, design reviews and working groups in the area of system performance.

## Sustainment

The contractor must support sustainment engineering activities for all systems in [*name of organization*]. The contractor must perform the following activities:

* Identify system components reaching end-of-life or end-of-service dates
* Identify and quantify the projected need for system components required to sustain current systems
* Identify and quantify projected shortfalls in system component availability or service life
* Identify technological opportunities that may provide alternatives for sustainment engineering

The contractor must support technical interchange meetings, design reviews and working groups in the area of sustainment engineering.

## Provisioning

The contractor must support provisioning engineering activities for all systems in [*name of organization*]. The contractor must perform the following activities:

* Support the development of a Maintenance Concept and support strategy
* Identify system components for which spares will be required, down to the Lowest Replaceable Unit (LRU)
* Identify those spares and quantities that will be on-site or off-site
* Coordinate with the Logistics Center for sparing strategies for each system
* Identify components that will require "life time buys" due to end-of-life/end-of-service date issues and the sparing strategy for those components
* Track system outages, time to restore service and spare usage to ensure sparing strategy is effective

The contractor must support technical interchange meetings, design reviews and working groups in the area of provisioning engineering, as well as Provisioning conferences.

## Investment Analysis

The contractor must provide systems engineering support for investment analysis activities (For more information go to http://fast.faa.gov, select Investment Analysis). The contractor must perform the following activities:

* Perform Market Surveys for new requirements and capabilities and evaluate each capability against the NAS architecture for technical and cost feasibility
* Perform Alternatives Analysis of available technologies to answer a Mission Need
* Perform Investment Analyses of each alternative, including returns on investment, comparisons among each alternative and ability to integrate the capability into the NAS

The contractor must provide reports and other documentation required to assist in the development of Acquisition Program Baseline documents and Joint Resource Council briefings.

## Quality Assurance

The contractor must provide systems engineering support for quality assurance activities. The contractor must provide support to [*name of organization*] and to FAA Quality Reliability Officers (QRO) assigned to each project within [*name of organization*].

The contractor must perform the following activities:

* Develop quality requirements for new system acquisitions
* Perform evaluations during site surveys and installations to assist the CO and QRO in determining contractor responsibility
* Evaluate proposals in the area of Quality Assurance
* Monitor performance in the area of Quality Assurance
* Develop rationale to support the acceptance or rejection of acquired systems, equipment and material in accordance with the system contract

The contractor must provide Quality Assurance support on-site in [*location of site*] and must support Quality Assurance activities at factory locations and test locations for each system developed for [*name of organization*].

Technology transfer is the process by which existing knowledge, facilities, or capabilities, developed with federal funding, can be transferred and used to fulfill public and private needs.

The Contractor must support [*name of organization*] in the following tasks:

* Identifying technology transfer activities and opportunities that can provide information, direct technical assistance, or subject matter experts to meet the needs of [*name of organization*]
* Publishing and disseminating information on the results of research performed in support of [*name of organization*] to government, universities, and industry.

## Risk Management

The contractor must provide systems engineering support in the area of risk management. The contractor must perform the following activities:

* Identify system risks
* Classify system risks as they apply to technical, cost, and/or schedule impacts
* Analyze and identify risk mitigation strategies
* Document all risks and track them to closure
* Provide periodic reports on risks, impacts and mitigation strategies, as required.

## System Security

The contractor must provide systems engineering support for system security. The contractor must perform the following activities:

* Provide support for the technical management of the definition, development, and evolution of systems security for all existing and new [*name of system(s)*] systems
* Manage the handling of all security-related data in accordance with FAA policy
* Prepare Security Plans that identify life cycle security engineering for *[name of systems(s)*] systems, as required
* Monitor the creation and evolution of Federal, departmental and agency security policy and guidance and assess their impact to [*name of system(s)*] systems
* Prepare and maintain System Certification and Authorization Packages (SCAP) as required, in accordance with policy and guidance provided by the FAA's Office of Automation Information Security (AIS) and FAA Order 1370.82
* Identify and document potential vulnerability risks and their mitigation strategies
* Provide support for the implementation of security risk mitigations
* Assess and monitor system security architecture and design, commercial security product selection, security testing, and any vulnerability mitigations for [*name of system(s)*] systems.

## Organizational Metrics

The contractor must assist [*name of organization*] in the formulation, preparation, tracking and reporting of organizational metrics. These metrics must determine the quantity and quality of services provided by [*name of organization*] in support of organizational and FAA goals. The contractor must provide mechanisms for the collection, reporting and dissemination of data needed for these metrics. The contractor must provide reports on organizational metrics on both a scheduled interval agreed to by [*name of organization*] and on an ad-hoc basis.

## Configuration Management

The contractor must support [*name of organization*] configuration management by providing knowledge and expertise to ensure that functional and physical characteristics of [*name of system(s)*] system configuration items meet the requirements during all phases of the systems life cycle. The contractor must review all specifications, contracts and statements of work to ensure requirements are properly and accurately documented in all contract configuration management related data requirements.

The contractor must provide project configuration management support as follows:

* Ensure that each project complies with FAA Order 1800.66
* Coordinate the accumulation of data configuration, cost, schedule, technical and operational impacts of proposed changes
* Review NAS Change Proposals (NCP) for impact on assigned projects, develop change summaries and make recommendations
* Review configuration changes, provide assessment of cost and schedule impact of such changes, and recommend when formal change proposals are required
* Collate data for formal changes; generate case files in the FAA status system, and follow-up resolutions of outstanding comments
* Provide executive support for Configuration Control Boards (CCB).
