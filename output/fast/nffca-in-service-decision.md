## Decision Criteria

* Operational capability demonstrated at key test site.
* Satisfactory operational test reports.
* Independent operational assessment for designated programs.
* Completed ISR Checklist.
* Approved safety risk management document.
* Information Systems Security Authorization.
* Stakeholder concurrence on readiness for in-service decision.
* ISD briefing and action plans.

## Decision Results

* Authorizes deployment of the solution into the operational environment.

## Who Does It

Service organization or program office presents the results of solution implementation.

ISD Secretariat manages the in-service decision-making process.

## Who Approves

In-service decision authority makes the in-service decision.

**Note:**The in-service decision authority may terminate the investment program or return it to solution implementation for additional work.
