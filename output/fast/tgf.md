# Target Generation Facility

TGF is on a mission to revolutionize the aviation industry by providing a dynamic simulation platform that mirrors real-time aircraft maneuvers across all flight phases. Our goal is to evolve the National Airspace System for a future of unparalleled safety and efficiency through realistic voice communications, pilot maneuvers, and airspace modeling.

In addition to being a robust simulation platform with high-fidelity, dynamic, Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) controlled targets, TGF also a driving force behind simulation services at the William J Hughes Technical Center for Advanced Aerospace. We oversee every aspect of the simulation lifecycle including lab connectivity, adaptation to scenario development, integration with external partners, and tool/visualization development. Additionally, we specialize in creating day-in-the-life simulations from historical data recordings, providing unparalleled insight and realism.

## History:

The history of TGF dates back to the early simulation capabilities of the Technical Center, where it has been providing traffic flows and voice responses since 1959. With a legacy built on decades of innovation and excellence, TGF continues to lead the way in aviation simulation.
