# Physical, Facility, and Personnel Security

[AMS Policy Section 4.11](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy4.11.pdf "AMS Policy Section 4.11")

The events of 911 demonstrated the crucial importance of physical, facility, and personnel security.

## What Must be Done

Plan, implement, and sustain physical, facility, and personnel security throughout the lifecycle of all FAA products and services including owned and leased facilities and aviation industry operations and activities.

## Who Does It

The service team or program office integrate physical, facility, and personnel security into the solution working with the Office of Security and Hazardous Materials.

## Who Approves

Document templates specify approval authorities.
