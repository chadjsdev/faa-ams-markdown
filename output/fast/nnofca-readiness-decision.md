## Decision Criteria

An Office of Information & Technology (AIT) Intake Form describing the project name,
estimated costs, problem/need description and project mapping and type must be submitted to the
Investment Management Process Division (AAP-200) and the Operations Governance Board (OGB) Secretariat 21 days prior
to the commitment of funding to any contract, task order,
or Inter-Agency Agreement (IAA) in support of the proposed project.  
The OGB will use a consensus model for decision-making.
At least one-half of the voting members must vote in order for decisions to be effective.
Decisions may be decided at the OGB meetings, or may be decided through electronic voting such as email.

## Decision Results

* Approves the governance path recommended by the OGB Secretariat and assigns an ART (if applicable)
  with representatives from relevant stakeholder organizations; or
* Selects an alternative governance path based on OGB consensus; or
* Returns the initiative to the sponsoring line of business for additional discovery
  (e.g., requirements definition or additional market research)

## Who Does It

* Service organization , Line of Business program office presents a synopsis of the AIT Intake Form
* OGB Secretariat manages the decision making as well as maintaining document control and artifact archives

## Who Approves

* OGB makes the Governance Path Readiness Decision
