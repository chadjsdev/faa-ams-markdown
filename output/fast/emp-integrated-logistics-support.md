# Integrated Logistics Support

[AMS Policy Section 4.3](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy4.3.pdf "AMS Policy Section 4.3")

Integrated logistics support activity plans, establishes, and sustains complete lifecycle support for FAA products and services.

## What Must be Done

Establish and sustain an integrated logistics support system throughout the lifecycle for all FAA products and services at optimal cost to the agency.

## Who Does It

The logistics manager leads the ILS management team in support of the service team or program office for all aspects of establishing and sustaining lifecycle support for operational products and services.

## Who Approves

Document templates specify approval authorities.
