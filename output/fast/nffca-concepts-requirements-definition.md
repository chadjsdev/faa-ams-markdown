### Concept & Requirements Definition

Translates priority operational needs in the enterprise architecture into preliminary requirements and a solution concept of operations for the capability needed to improve service delivery. Quantifies the service shortfall in sufficient detail for the definition of realistic preliminary requirements and the estimation of potential costs and benefits. Identifies the most promising alternative solutions able to satisfy the service need, one of which must be consistent with the conceptual framework in the enterprise architecture.

## [What Must be Done](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.4.pdf#nameddest=policy2_4_1 "What Must be Done")

Define solution Conops and preliminary requirements; identify safe, secure, and affordable alternative solutions that satisfy functional and performance requirements; validate maturity of technology and requirements; designate acquisition category; plan for investment analysis.

## [Who Does It](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.4.pdf#nameddest=policy2_4_3 "Who Does It")

Service organization leads and completes all CRD activities assisted by NextGen, ATO PMO, and AFN IT organizations.

## [Who Approves](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.4.pdf#nameddest=policy2_4_4 "Who Approves")

Acquisition Executive Board concurs with the initiative's ACAT recommendation. The FAA Acquisition Executive approves. (JRC concurs)

SASP/CRD Guidelines specify CRD artifact approvals.
