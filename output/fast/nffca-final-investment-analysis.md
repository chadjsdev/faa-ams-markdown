### Final Investment Analysis

Coordinate with important stakeholders to finalize strategy for implementation and lifecycle support for the solution. Include at a minimum end users, maintainers, logistics, training, testing and evaluation, safety, and security. Solicit and evaluate offers for the solution and use findings to finalize program costs, benefits, risks, planning, and schedule. Conduct economic analysis. Develop, verify, and validate required AMS outputs and products. Finalize risk mitigation strategies. Plan for deployment and post implementation review.

## [What Must be Done](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.5.pdf#nameddest=policy2_5_1 "What Must be Done")

Finalize and validate program requirements, business case and implementation strategy. Solicit and evaluate offers from the industry. Develop detailed plans and a cost/schedule/performance baseline or execution plan for the solution.

## [Who Does It](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.5.pdf#nameddest=policy2_5_3 "Who Does It")

The providing service organization or program office leads an investment analysis team comprised of operational experts, key stakeholders, engineering and technical specialists, business analysts, and program planning and management specialists.

## [Who Approves](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.5.pdf#nameddest=policy2_5_4 "Who Approves")

Document templates specify approval authorities.

Joint Resources Council makes the final investment decision.
