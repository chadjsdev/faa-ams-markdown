The program office will be responsible for obtaining these signatures prior to the FID. For Air Traffic Organization programs, the head of the sponsoring organization (if different from the managing organization) must approve the Acquisition Program Baseline.

*The Acquisition Program Baseline (APB) documents the cost, schedule, and performance parameters for the acquisition program. Risk to meeting these parameters are tracked and reported.*

*The APB is the mutual agreement between the Joint Resources Council (JRC), the managing organization, and the sponsoring organization.*

*The APB is jointly developed by the Program Team and the Capital Program Formulation Branch (ABP-310) of the Financial Services organization (ABA).*

*The APB is finalized prior to the Final Investment Decision (FID). The APB encompasses summary level cost, schedule, and performance data from key artifacts as required by the AMS ACATs, which may include the final Program Requirements Document, Business Case, and Implementation Strategy and Planning Document.*

**Program Description**

*Briefly describe the program. The overview should be a summary of the detailed program description and the scope of work included in the investment decision.*

**Cost**

*The Cost Baseline includes the total Facilities and Equipment (F&E) costs for planning, acquisition, and implementation, Activities 1 – 4 in the F&E appropriation. The total cost establishes the Baseline Cost at Completion (BCAC) for the program. The cost baseline does not include government personnel compensation, benefits, and travel (PCB&T) also known as Activity 5 funds or the Operations (Ops) appropriation.*
