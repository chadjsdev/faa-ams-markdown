## Decision Criteria

* The scaled business case will be reviewed to ensure the initiative presents an opportunity to improve
  operating conditions and is a sensible and reasonable
  use of proposed funding

## Decision Results

* Approves the investment to enter solution development to complete planning artifacts along with any additional Operations Governance Board (OGB)-directed requirements, or
* Returns the investment to alternatives analysis to refine or modify planning artifacts, or
* Recommends the initiative be paused, modified, or cancelled

## Who Does It

* Service organization/Line of Business program office and/or the Acquisition Readiness Team (ART) present the Scaled Business Case
* OGB Secretariat manages the decision making as well as maintaining document control and artifact archives

## Who Approves

OGB approves the Scaled Business Case.
