# Operations Governance Board (OGB)

[OGB   
 Charter](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/ogbcharter.pdf "Ops Gov Board Charter")

Operations Governance Board (OGB) through delegation from the Joint Resources Council (JRC), OGB is the investment decision authority for Mission Support Operations (OPS) funded capital investments.
Mission Support Ops Funded initiatives have each of the following three attributes. They are:

### Mission Support

* Investments not included in the NAS Enterprise Architecture
* Investments included in the Mission Support Enterprise Architecture
* Investments not included in either architecture,
  but deemed within scope by the OGB or JRC

### Ops Funded

* Investments that providers intend to fund entirely from the Operations and Maintenance account

### Capital Investment

* Acquisition of a new or modernized FAA product, system, or service that results in a capital asset such as land, structures, equipment, or intellectual property (including software)
* Typically has a useful life of two years or more
* Generally does not include investments associated with the repair,
  operation, or maintenance of previously fielded assets

It has permanent members from the following organizations:

* Acquisition (ACQ)
* Investment Planning & Analysis (IP&A)
* Office of the Chief Counsel (AGC)
* Air Traffic Organization (ATO)
* Office of Aviation Safety (AVS)
* Information and Technology Services (AIT)

Representatives from the appropriate Line-Of-Business (LOB) attend OGB meetings on an ad hoc basis when the investment initiative relates to their business or organizational interests.
The Line-Of-Business members who may attend as needed are:

* Air Traffic Organization (ATO) Property Management Officer (PMO)
* ATO organizations outside PMO
* Office of Human Resources (AHR)
* Airports (ARP)
* Office of Security & Hazardous Materials (ASH)
* International Affairs, and Environment (APL)
