# Office of Information & Technology

The Assistant Administrator for Information & Technology is the FAA’s Chief Information Officer (CIO). The CIO is the principal adviser to the Administrator and to FAA organizations on matters involving IT management and governance and has primary FAA oversight responsibility for all Agency IT investments.

The Office of Information & Technology provides the following services:

* Strategy & Performance
* Mission Partnership
* Solution Delivery
* Infrastructure & Operations
* Information Security & Privacy
