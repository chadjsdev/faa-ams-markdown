## What Must be Done

* The Acquisition Readiness Team (ART) reviews and revises
  as necessary the required artifacts verifying their
  completeness and accuracy
* The Operations Governance Board (OGB) Secretariat records the artifacts as complete
  and maintains the final copies

## Who Does It

* The line of business with the service need in collaboration with the ART
* OGB Secretariat

## Who Approves

The OGB reviews the final scaled business case during Investment Commitment Decision.
