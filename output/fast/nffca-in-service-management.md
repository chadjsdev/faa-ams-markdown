## [What Must be Done](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.7.pdf#nameddest=policy2_7_1 "What Must be Done")

Sustain and execute in-service planning; deliver and sustain services; monitor performance and customer expectations; plan for and obtain upgrades when needed; end service life and dispose of assets when no longer needed.

## [Who Does It](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.7.pdf#nameddest=policy2_7_3 "Who Does It")

Service organization monitors service performance and oversees long-range service delivery planning.

ATO Technical Operations keeps operational assets working and reports performance.

FAA Technical Center develops, tests, and deploys changes to operational assets to correct issues and trouble reports.

FAA Logistics Center manages the supply chain and provides depot, logistics and training services.

PIR Quality Officer oversees planning, conduct, and reporting of post-implementation reviews.
