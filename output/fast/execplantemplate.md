The program office will be responsible for obtaining these signatures prior to the investment decision. For Air Traffic Organization programs, the head of the sponsoring organization (if different from the managing organization) must approve the Execution Plan.

For updates to Tech Refresh Portfolio (TRP) Execution Plans, the only signatures required are the Portfolio/Program Manager, Stakeholder’s Governance Body Chairperson, Director, and FAA Acquisition Executive (FAE).

*The Execution Plan (EP) establishes the cost, schedule, and performance parameters within which the program is authorized. Risk to meeting these parameters are tracked and reported.*

*The EP is a formal document approved by the JRC at the Investment Decision and is the implementation contract between the FAA and the service organization acquiring an approved product or service. EPs are used for FAA acquisition baseline variance measurement and internal variance reporting (when applicable). Execution plans are not subject to* [*US Code §40121*](#_Legal_Basis) *reporting requirements.*

*The Execution Plan is jointly developed by the program team and Capital Program Formulation branch (ABP-310) of the Financial Services organization (ABA).*

*The Execution Plan encompasses summary level cost, schedule, and performance parameters from key artifacts as required by the AMS ACATs.*

**Cost**

*The Cost Baseline includes the total Facilities and Equipment (F&E) costs for planning, acquisition, and implementation, Activities 1 – 4 in the F&E appropriation. The cost baseline does not include government personnel compensation, benefits, and travel (PCB&T) also known as Activity 5 funds or the Operations (Ops) appropriation.  Exceptions include facility projects that are funded with Ops, these are typically FAA administrative buildings. The costs should be consistent with the Rough Lifecycle Cost Estimate or Rough Order of Magnitude (ROM) cost and/or Final Business Case, as appropriate for the Acquisition Category (ACAT).*

*Follow the guidance below from the applicable Cost type:*

1. *Standard – The standard cost table is used for the following ACATs:*
   * *New Investment - Level IIA Enhancement, Level IIB Expansion*

   * *Tech Refresh Portfolio*
   * *Variable Quantity*

*For P/P/P that require two funding levels, the EP documents the affordable and requested levels.*

* + *Affordable Level – The affordable level is typically the funding level recommended by the Capital Investment Team (CIT).*
  + *Requested Level – The requested level documents the programs full requirements that can be met within current FAA staffing levels and is executable by the program.  The requested level is typically higher than the affordable level.*

**Note(s)**

**Schedule**

*The schedule baseline consists of milestones selected from the* [*<u>Acquisition Management System (AMS) Standard system or Facility Milestones</u>*](https://fast.faa.gov/NFFCA_Decision_Reviews_StdMilestones.cfm)*. List as well as appropriate milestones developed in cases where the AMS Standard Milestones do not apply. The schedule duration is measured in months and is the period from the first milestone to the last milestone. This establishes the Baseline Schedule at Completion (BSAC). In cases where the AMS standard milestones do not apply, the Program Office (PO) should work with the Office of Budget and Programs, Capital Program Formulation Branch to develop a set of tailored, measurable, and comparable milestones to be used in the schedule baseline.*

*Schedule Table Guidance:*

* *Milestone: Description of the milestone.*
* *AMS Code: Applicable system or facility* [*<u>milestone code</u>*](https://fast.faa.gov/NFFCA_Decision_Reviews_StdMilestones.cfm)*.*
* *Flag: The flag will be determined by the Capital Program Formulation Branch to identify the first (F), last (L), or independent (I) milestones, which is used to determine the total baseline duration.*
* *Baseline Date: This is the completion date of the milestone, represented in month and year.*
* *Duration: The duration for each milestone represents the cumulative duration from the first milestone to the subject milestone. This is used to establish your total baseline duration.*
* *Note Reference: Notes defining the completion criteria for milestones will be documented in the notes section or any applicable notes to schedule milestones. A notes reference number will be used to align with the notes table below.*

*Tech Refresh Portfolio (TRP) Guidance- The TRP EP will document the first and last milestone in the schedule section establishing the total schedule duration for the portfolio baseline, which typically aligns with the cost profile. In addition, interim milestones, milestones between the first and last milestones, are developed and cover the first 1-2 years of the portfolio. Every 1-2 years (no more than 2 years) the interim milestones are updated by the Stakeholder Governance Body (SGB) with concurrence from the Capital Program Formulation Branch and FAE approval.*
