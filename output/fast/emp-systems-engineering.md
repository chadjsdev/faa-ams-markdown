# Systems Engineering

[AMS Policy Section 4.10](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy4.10.pdf "AMS Policy Section 4.10")

Systems engineering is conducted throughout the lifecycle management process at many levels from individual investment programs to the National Airspace System as a whole.

## What Must be Done

Develop and institute a systems engineering management program.

## Who Does It

All organizations responsible for the development, implementation, integration, and lifecycle management of investment assets.

## Who Approves

Each investment and engineering organization approves its systems engineering management program.

Document templates specify approval authorities.
