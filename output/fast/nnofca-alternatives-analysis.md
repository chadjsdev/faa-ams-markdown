## What Must be Done

* The Line of Business sponsoring the initiative and Acquisition Readiness Team (ART) begins to create the required artifacts as well produce a scaled business case
* ART ensures the alternative emerging from the analysis fits within the overall agency strategy for obtaining the operational capability and can provide the performance and functionality needed by users

## Who Does It

* The line of business with the service need in collaboration with the
  ART

## Who Approves

* The Operations Governance Board (OGB) approves the preliminary scaled business
  case and other required planning artifacts at the Business Case Decision point
