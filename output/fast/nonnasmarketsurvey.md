| 1. **Document Purpose** |
| The purpose of the Government and Market Survey is to help the customer identify and analyze a wide variety of investment options across the government and general market to ensure the best available solution is selected. |

| **Identify and briefly describe alternatives that were considered in planning for this solution.** |
| *Response should identify all major alternative solutions that were considered, what vendors would have been used to provide the solutions, and describe key characteristics and shortcomings of alternative solutions.* |
| **Describe the solution that was selected and explain why it was selected over the alternatives.** |
| *Response should describe the selected solution and address how it meets mandatory requirements, ease of acquisition and use, availability within FAA’s Enterprise Architecture, etc… Response should address the entire solution, including but not limited to, technology, hosting, staffing, and configuration.* |
| **Has a proof of concept been completed? [ ]  Yes [ ]  No** |
