### Initial Investment Analysis

Coordinate and work with important stakeholders to include end users, maintainers, business case analysts, logistics, training, testing and evaluation, safety, and security. Synchronize with dependent capabilities. Identify risks and develop mitigations strategies. Investigate possible solutions and generate information enabling the Joint Resources Council to select the alternative that best satisfies FAA performance goals and customer service needs within cost, operational, performance, benefit, and risk constraints. Develop, verify, and validate required AMS outputs and products.

## [What Must be Done](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.5.pdf#nameddest=policy2_5_1 "What Must be Done")

Form an investment analysis team, evaluate the business case and affordability of each alternative solution, develop/validate key outputs and products, plan for final investment analysis, and prepare for the initial investment decision.

## [Who Does It](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.5.pdf#nameddest=policy2_5_3 "Who Does It")

Performed by an investment analysis team comprised of operational experts, key stakeholders, engineering and technical specialists, business analysts, and providers.

## [Who Approves](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.5.pdf#nameddest=policy2_5_4 "Who Approves")

Document templates specify approval authorities.

Joint Resources Council makes the initial investment decision.
