## What Must be Done

Intake Form describing the project name,
Intake Form estimated costs, problem/need description and project mapping and type
must be submitted to the Investment Management Process Division (AAP-200) and
the Operations Governance Board (OGB) Secretariat at least 21 days prior to the commitment of funding to any contract,
task order, or Inter-Agency Agreement (IAA) in support of the proposed project.

## Who Does It

* The line of business with the service need

## Who Approves

AAP-200 will review intake form and provide the OGB with a recommendation for project governance path.
The OGB determines the proper governance path and approves the initiative to begin that path.
