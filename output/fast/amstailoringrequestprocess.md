# AMSTailoringRequestProcess

**Acquisition Management System**

**Tailoring Request Process**

**Background**

Organizations managing investment initiatives may request tailoring of the Acquisition Management System (AMS) lifecycle management process by submitting a tailoring request to the Acquisition Executive Board (AEB) before the investment analysis readiness decision (IARD) or other Joint Resource Council (JRC) investment decision.

**Decision Authority**

The AEB reviews the tailoring request and can concur with the request, deny the request or ask for changes to the request.  Upon AEB concurrence, the tailoring request is forwarded to the FAA Acquisition Executive (FAE) for review and approval.  If approved, the FAE signs the tailoring request.  The AEB is given an opportunity to rationalize tailoring decisions before potential overrule by the FAE.

Any subsequent actions by stakeholders or the program office to revise or modify previous AEB tailoring decisions must be presented to the AEB for concurrence and approval by the FAE.

**Tailoring Process**

**Step 1: Contact AEB Secretariat and Schedule Preparatory Meeting**

The Investment sponsor contacts the AEB Secretariat to discuss the initiative, the proposed tailoring and waiver requests, and the justifications for them.  It is also strongly recommended that the investment sponsor schedule a meeting with the AEB Secretariat to discuss the tailor and waiver request process.  

**Step 2: Prepare and Submit Tailoring Request Form**

The investment sponsor fills out the following AMS tailoring request template and sends it to the AEB Secretariat (AAP-100) for processing.  See the tailoring request template for instructions on content of the request.  

**Step 3: Present Tailoring Request to the AEB**

The AEB Secretariat schedules presentation of the tailoring request at the first available AEB meeting with an agenda opening. The investment sponsor ensures their AEB member is aware of the tailoring request and when it will be presented.  The investment sponsor (or the cognizant AEB member) presents the tailoring request and rationale to the AEB. 

**Step 4: Record AEB Decision**

The AEB Secretariat records the AEB decision and any conditions imposed by the AEB at the meeting.  The AEB Secretariat obtains the signature of the FAE, signifying approval. 

**Step 5: Send Tailoring Decision to the JRC Secretariat**

The AEB Secretariat sends the tailoring decision to the JRC Secretariat for use in the JRC Readiness Process. 

**Step 6: As Necessary, Present Tailoring Rationale to the FAE**

If the FAE intends to disapprove the request, the AEB and investment sponsor present the rationale for the tailoring request and concurrence to the FAE.

**Requested AMS Tailoring**

*The contents of this section should describe the necessary steps proposed by your team, to obtain the pursued investment decision.  Following the criteria established in the AMS, describe how your program intends to meet the AMS Investment Decision requirements that you are pursuing.  Provide the list of artifacts for the assigned ACAT that are required for this AMS Decision Point.  Describe how you plan to meet each one of the applicable steps and corresponding artifact requirements.  Indicate what type of waiver/tailoring action the team is requesting in each one of the steps with the rational for approval.*

*There are several types of tailoring actions that can be solicited through a waiver/tailoring request. In terms of AMS Lifecycle Investment decisions and processes, a waiver is a request to eliminate a required artifact or even an entire AMS phase or decision point.  A tailoring request is an appeal to submit an alternate artifact or document in lieu of an AMS requirement such as modifying the contents or scope of a required analysis or document or the use of equivalent analysis in lieu of the formats or standards called for in the AMS.  However, all actions must be properly justified with a sound rationale and the modifications still must meet the spirit of intent of the original AMS decision or requirement.*

Note: Use the Joint Resource Council (JRC) Readiness Criteria and Checklist to cross check and verify that you have considered all the basic decision requirements. Examples of previously submitted Tailoring Requests are available upon request.
