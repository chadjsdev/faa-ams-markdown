### Initial Investment Decision

The Joint Resources Council confirms coordination has occurred with all-important stakeholders and that key risks and issues have effective mitigation strategies in place. The JRC then selects the alternative solution that best satisfies FAA performance goals and customer service needs within cost, operational, performance, benefit, and risk constraints, or rejects all alternatives and specifies what action is needed next.

## Decision Criteria

* Lifecycle costs
* Benefits
* Benefit-to-cost ratio
* Risk
* Consistency with the FAA Enterprise Architecture
* Impact on FAA strategic goals

## Decision Results

* Selects an alternative for detailed implementation planning.
* Approves entry into final investment analysis.
* Approves funding for analytical or developmental work related to the selected alternative.
* Designates a service organization to lead final investment analysis and be responsible for solution implementation.

## Who Does It

Service organization or program office presents the results of initial investment analysis.

JRC Secretariat manages the JRC decision-making process.

## Who Approves

Joint Resources Council makes the initial investment decision.

**Note:**The JRC may reject the recommendation of the service organization or program office and return the initiative for additional work or termination.
