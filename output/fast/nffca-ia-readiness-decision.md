### Investment Analysis Readiness Decision

The Joint Resources Council confirms proposals are consistent with overall corporate needs and planning. The decision is made within context of all ongoing and planned investment activities to sustain and improve service delivery. The JRC confirms all JRC checklist items are completed. The Joint Resources Council makes the decision to enter investment analysis.

## Decision Criteria

* Mature technology and requirements
* Realistic alternative solutions
* Approved shortfall analysis report
* Updated enterprise architecture products
* Signed plan for investment analysis

## Decision Results

A decision to enter investment analysis with funding and staff for the investment analysis team or a decision to return the initiative for additional work or termination.

## Who Does It

Service organization or program office presents the results of concept and requirements definition.

JRC Secretariat manages the JRC decision-making process.

## Who Approves It

Joint Resources Council makes the investment analysis readiness decision.
