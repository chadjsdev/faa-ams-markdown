# Quality Assurance

[AMS Policy Section 3.10.4](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy3.10.pdf#nameddest=policy3_10_4 "AMS Policy Section 3.10.4")

Quality assurance policy and guidance apply to the procurement of all systems, equipment, material, and services.

## What Must be Done

Ensure contractors enact appropriate quality assurance requirements to obtain quality products and services.

## Who Does It

Service organizations or program offices plan, fund, and obtain quality assurance on their investment programs.

Quality Reliability Officer oversees in-plant quality for NAS systems.

## Who Approves

Quality Assurance Office ensures appropriate quality assurance requirements are implemented for NAS systems.
