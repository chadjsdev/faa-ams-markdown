## What Must be Done

* Operate, maintain, secure and sustain systems and services
* Monitor performance and customer expectations
* Plan for and obtain upgrades when needed
* End service life and dispose of assets when no longer needed

## Who Does It

* Service organization monitors service performance and
  oversees long-range service planning

## Who Approves

* Program office makes day to day decisions

## Key Outputs and Products

* Operational capability maintained at each site throughout service life as well as during retirement
* Operational capability Replaced or removed at each site as needed
