# FacilityMilestones

**AMS Standard Facility Milestones**

|  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Milestone** | **Milestone Description** | **AMS Phase** | **Decision Authority** |
| F1 | **Concept and requirements definition readiness decision:** The enterprise architecture roadmaps prioritize and describe capability shortfalls and opportunities for the FAA. All investment opportunities that enter the AMS lifecycle must be in response to a priority service need found in an enterprise architecture roadmap. | Service analysis & Strategic Planning | FAA Enterprise Architecture Board |
| F2 | **Preliminary program requirements (pPR) approved:** Preliminary program requirements are developed during concept and requirements definition and recorded in the preliminary document program requirements (pPRD). Program requirements are revised during initial investment analysis and finalized during final investment analysis. | Concept & Requirements Definition | ATO: Vice Presidents  of the implementing and operating service organizations  Other LOBs: 2nd level executive of the implementing service organization |
| F3 | **Initial investment analysis plan approved:** This plan defines what will be done during initial investment analysis and the participating organizations. It is approved before the readiness for investment analysis decision. | Concept & Requirements Definition | Director, Investment Planning & Analysis   ATO: Vice President of the service organization with the need   Other LOB: Director of the service organization with the need |
| F4 | **Investment analysis readiness decision:** This decision occurs after completion of concept and requirements definition. It verifies that preliminary requirements are well-defined and identifies the preliminary alternatives that will be evaluated during initial investment analysis. | Concept & Requirements Definition | Joint Resources Council |
| F5 | **Preliminary business case approved:** The investment analysis team prepares the preliminary business case during initial investment analysis. The business case defines the investment alternatives and quantifies the costs and benefits associated with each. | Initial Investment Analysis | Vice president (ATO) or Director (non-ATO) of the service organization with the service need |
| F6 | **Program requirements document update approved:** Program requirements are revised to reflect the results of initial investment analysis. | Initial Investment Analysis | ATO: Vice Presidents  of the implementing and operating service organizations  Other LOBs: 2nd level executive of the implementing service organization |
| F7 | **Final investment analysis plan approved:** The investment analysis plan team defines the actions, resources, schedules, roles and responsibilities, documentation, and exit criteria for final investment analysis. The plan includes any risk-reduction effort that may be necessary such as modeling, analysis, simulation, or research. | Initial Investment Analysis | Director, Investment Planning & Analysis   ATO: Vice President of the service organization with the need   Other LOB: Director of the service organization with the need |
| F8 | **Initial investment decision:** The Joint Resources Council selects a solution for implementation at the initial investment decision and authorizes entry into final investment analysis for detailed planning and development of the acquisition program baseline. | Initial Investment Analysis | Joint Resources Council |
| F9 | **Facility specification approved****:** The specification is approved before issuing a screening information request for architectural design. | Final investment analysis | Service team leader |
| F10 | **Screening information request for facility architectural design released:** Vendor responses are a basis for cost and schedule baselines in the APB. | Final investment analysis | Service team contracting officer |
| F11 | **Final program requirements document approved:** Final approved program requirements are required for the final investment decision. | Final investment analysis | ATO: Vice Presidents  of the implementing and operating service organizations  Other LOBs: 2nd level executive of the implementing service organization |
| F12 | **Final business case approved:** A revised business case is required for the final investment decision. The final business case reflects final costs, benefits, schedule, and performance for the alternative selected for implementation. | Final investment analysis | The Vice President (ATO) or Director (non-ATO) of the organization with the service need |
| F13 | **Implementation strategy and planning document (ISPD) approved:** The ISPD defines the overall planning by which the service team will execute the facility program during solution implementation and support fielded products during in-service management. | Final investment analysis | See the ISPD template or AMS Appendix B for approval authorities |
| F14 | **Site-selection/acquisition assessment completed:** Includes collection of all site-selection data such as: real estate appraisals and zoning restrictions, legal concerns such as title search and property status, and environmental assessments. Costs and schedules are included in the ISPD and acquisition program baseline. | Final investment analysis | Service team leader |
| F15 | **Final acquisition program baseline approved:** The acquisition program baseline establishes the performance, cost, schedule and benefits boundaries for implementing an investment program. The investment analysis team develops the APB during final investment analysis for the solution selected for implementation at the initial investment decision. The service team that will implement the investment program develops cost and schedule baselines. | Final investment analysis | Acquisition executive and members of the Joint Resources Council |
| F16 | **Final investment decision:** The Joint Resources Council approves and baselines an investment program at the final investment decision. | Final investment analysis | Joint Resources Council |
| F17 | **Facility conceptual design completed:** For a major facility the conceptual design effort encompasses an architectural and engineering contract of approximately one year with incremental design reviews at 30%, 50% and 100%. When all review comments are incorporated into design plans and specifications, the conceptual design is completed. | Solution Implementation | Service team leader and site engineer |
| F18 | **Memoranda of agreement with equipment suppliers Completed:** The facility program office has completed MOAs with all equipment/software suppliers (service teams) that the facility will house; e.g., automation, communications, surveillance systems. | Solution Implementation | Service team leader |
| F19 | **Contract(s) for site-specific detailed design awarded:** Using the approved conceptual design, award site-specific detailed design contracts to architecture/engineering firms to adapt the design to specific sites that will be used in the Invitation For Bid for facility construction. | Solution Implementation | Service area contracting officer |
| F20 | **Facility design completed:** The site-specific detailed design of the facility, including plans and specifications, is 100% completed. | Solution Implementation | Site engineer or designated official |
| F21 | **Site(s) Acquisition:** Site(s) selected, procurements negotiated, and real estate purchase completed. | Solution Implementation | Service area contracting officer |
| F22 | **Facility procurement request(s) to service-area logistics office completed:** The service area engineering office in consonance with the service area logistics office completes the procurement request(s) and submits it (them) to the contracting officer for construction of the facility(ies). The procurement request(s) is accompanied by  plans, specifications, statement of work, etc. | Solution Implementation | Service team leader |
| F23 | **Invitation(s) for bid (IFB) issued:** Building construction funds have been received by the service-area office and prospective bidders are sent an Invitation for Bid, which includes a complete procurement package (construction plans, specifications, statement of work, and standard procurement documentation). | Solution Implementation | Service team leader |
| F24 | **Regional construction contracts awarded:** Bid opening has been completed, bidder price has been verified, the bidder has been determined to be responsive, and the contract is awarded. | Solution Implementation | Service-area contracting officer |
| F25 | **Facility construction begun:** The contracting officer has issued a Notice to Proceed to the successful bidder. | Solution Implementation | Service-area contracting officer |
| F26 | **Facility construction completed:** The facility has been inspected and is determined to be in compliance with the contract and is approved for FAA occupancy. | Solution Implementation | Service-area site engineer |
| F27 | **Beneficial occupancy date****:** The date when a pre-selected portion of the building or a new facility is occupied for FAA use; e.g., electronic equipment rooms, operations room. | Solution Implementation | Service-area site engineer |
