## [Scope](https://fast.faa.gov/NNOFCA_Scope.cfm "Scope")

The Mission Support Ops funded process applies to investment initiatives with the following
three attributes: Mission Support, Ops Funded, Capital Investment.

## [Governance Paths](https://fast.faa.gov/NNOFCA_Governance_Paths.cfm "Governance Paths")

Each initiative is given a Governance Path to follow by the Operations Governance Board (OGB) at the Readiness Decision.

## [Operations Governance Board (OGB)](https://fast.faa.gov/NNOFCA_Operations_Governance_Board.cfm "Operations Governance Board")

Performs investment oversight of Mission Support Ops Funded capital acquisitions.
Through delegation from the Joint Resources Council (JRC) the OGB becomes the Investment Decision-making Authority (IDA) for Mission Support Ops Funded capital acquisitions.

## [Required Artifacts](https://fast.faa.gov/NNOFCA_Required_Toolkit.cfm "Required Artifacts")

Each initiative is required to complete key artifacts as directed by the OGB.
The creation and review of each of these checklist items and the control document is determined by the initiative governance path.
