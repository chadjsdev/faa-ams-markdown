### Concept & Requirements Definition Readiness Decision

Occurs when the FAA Enterprise Architecture Board verifies: (1) the service shortfall, operational improvement, or operational sustainment is in an enterprise architecture roadmap; and (2) planning and resources for concept and requirements definition are in place. The readiness decision is the gateway between service analysis and strategic planning and concept and requirements definition.

## Decision Criteria

Service shortfall or operational improvement or sustainment is in an Enterprise Architecture roadmap and is a compelling need of the FAA.

Approved plan and resources for concept & requirements definition.

## Decision Results

Approval to enter concept and requirements definition or return the initiative for additional work or termination.

## Who Does It

Service organization or program office presents the results of service analysis.

JRC Secretariat manages the decision-making process.

## Who Approves

FAA Enterprise Architecture Board makes the CRD readiness decision.
