# Deployment Planning

[AMS Policy Section 4.6](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy4.6.pdf "AMS Policy Section 4.6 ")

Deployment planning prepares for and assesses the readiness of a
solution to be implemented into its operational environment via the in-service decision.

## What Must be Done

Deployment planning involves coordination among critical planning, engineering, and
support disciplines to ensure fielded assets meet functional and performance requirements
and can be supported throughout their service life.

## Who Does It

Implementing service organization is responsible for all deployment planning activities.

Operating service organization provides technical assistance.

## Who Approves

Document templates specify approval authorities.
