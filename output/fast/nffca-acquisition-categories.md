# Acquisition Categories

[AMS Policy Section 1.2.5](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy1.2.pdf#nameddest=policy1_2_5 "AMS Policy Section 1.2.5")

Acquisition categories establish tailored governance and artifact requirements for investment programs based on specific qualitative and quantitative criteria.

## What Must be Done

Every FAA investment program is classified by investment type and then categorized based on cost, complexity, risk, safety, security, and political sensitivity.

## Who Does It

The sponsoring service organization recommends an acquisition category to the Acquisition Executive Board early in the concept and requirements definition phase.

## Who Approves

The Acquisition Executive Board makes the determination and notifies the Joint Resources Council via the JRC Secretariat.
