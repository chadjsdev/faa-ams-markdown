# Operational Analysis

[AMS Policy Section 4.15.2](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy4.15.pdf#nameddest=policy4_15_2 "AMS Policy Section 4.15.2")

The safety, efficiency, & effectiveness of operational assets are evaluated periodically as a basis for sustaining & improving service delivery over time.

## What Must be Done

Gather, analyze, and report availability, reliability, maintenance, and performance data; evaluate data
to determine whether the operational asset can continue to meet user service needs; plan and execute corrective
action when it cannot.

## Who Does It

The Director of the performing service organization is responsible for conducting operational analysis.

## Who Approves

Operational analysis guidance specifies how to gather, analyze & report results.
