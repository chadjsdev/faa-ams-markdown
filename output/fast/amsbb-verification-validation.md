# Verification & Validation

[AMS Policy Section 2.1.6](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.1.pdf#nameddest=policy2_1_6 "AMS Policy Section 2.1.6")

Verification and validation are performed throughout the AMS lifecycle to support investment decision-making and make sure the right products are built right.

## What Must be Done

Plan and execute V&V activities during each lifecycle management phase for key work products, product components, and end products.

## Who Does It

Program offices and service organizations.

## Who Approves

In-service decision authority approves end product.

Templates specify document approval authorities.

## Key Outputs and Products

Key Outputs and Products

| AMS Phase | Key Work Products |
| --- | --- |
| Service Analysis & Strategic Planning | Preliminary shortfall analysis, CRD plan |
| Concept & Requirements Definition | Solution ConOps, preliminary PRD, EA products & amendments, shortfall analysis, investment analysis plan |
| Initial Investment Analysis | Updated PRD, initial business case, initial ISPD, final investment analysis plan |
| Final Investment Analysis | Final PRD, final business case, final ISPD, Acquisition Program Baseline, tailored ISR Checklist, Screening Information Request |
| Solution Implementation | Contract and specification, design documents, product demonstration decision (if applicable), end-product or service |
