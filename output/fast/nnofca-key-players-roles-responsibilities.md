# Key Players, Roles & Responsibilities

[AMS Policy Section 2.8](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.8.pdf "AMS Policy Section 2.8")

Executive Level Key Players

| Executive Level | |
| --- | --- |
| Joint Resources Council (JRC) | * Oversight authority for all capital investments at FAA - whether Operations (OPS), Research & Development (R&D), Airport Improvement Program (AIP), or Facilities & Equipment (F&E) funded * Delegates Mission Support strategy development to the ITSSC; and Mission Support, Ops Funded investment decision-making to the Operations Governance Board (OGB) * May delegate other investments as deemed appropriate. |
| OGB | * Performs investment oversight of Mission Support, Ops Funded capital acqusitions * Executes Mission Support startegy through oversight and investment decision-making * Documents and disseminates investment performance parameters |

  

Management Level Key Players

| Management Level | |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition Readiness Team (ART) | * Collaborates with customers to develop decision-ready investment packages for Mission Support, Ops Funded IT investments |
| OGB Secretariat | * Manage the Operations Support Pathway process from start to finish for each investment. * Responsible for filtering potential investments by making Governance Path recommendation to the OGB * Assesses potential investments on a series of factors |
| Business Partnership Manager (BPM) | * Serves as the "front door" of Information and Technology Services (AIT) * Manages customer relationships on behalf of AIT - providing up-to-date information to AIT services and anticipating customer needs * Serves as the focal point in capturing and initially documenting new customer needs |
| Customer | * Any FAA organization seeking to execute Mission Support capital acquisition using Operations funds |
