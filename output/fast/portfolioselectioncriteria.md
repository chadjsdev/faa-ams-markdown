***Stage 3 Portfolio Selection Criteria***

The investment analysis team ranks investment alternatives and presents results to the JRC at the initial investment decision. “N” represents the number of alternatives***. If the investment initiative is an element of an operational capability, alternatives must also provide the performance and functionality necessary to achieve the operational capability.***
