# NonNASCapitalInvestmentsManagementPlan

*[INVESTMENT NAME]*INVESTMENT MANAGEMENT PLAN

|  |
| --- |
| 1. **Document Purpose** |
| The purpose of the Investment Management Plan is to document plans for implementing the project from contract award to final acceptance and use by the end users. This includes identifying primary deliverables, milestones, stakeholders, risk management, and change management activities. |
