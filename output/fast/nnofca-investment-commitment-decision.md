## Decision Criteria

* The scaled business case will be reviewed to ensure the initiative presents an opportunity to improve
  Operating conditions and is a sensible and reasonable
  use of proposed funding

## Decision Results

The Operations (OPS) Governance Board reviews the final scaled business case and other
planning artifacts ensuring the investment presents an opportunity to
improve operating capability, satisfy customer service needs,
and is a sensible use of agency resources. It then:

* Approves the project to enter deployment, or
* Returns the initiative to the solution development phase for additional work, or
* Recommends the initiative for termination

## Who Does It

* Service organization, Line of Business program office, and the
  Acquisition Readiness Team (ART) present the Final Business Case
* OGB Secretariat manages the decision making process

## Who Approves

* The OPS Governance Board reviews the final scaled business case and other planning artifacts
