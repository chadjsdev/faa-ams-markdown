# Environmental, Occupational Safety & Health, and Energy Considerations

[AMS Policy Section 4.8](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy4.8.pdf "AMS Policy Section 4.8")

Environmental, occupational safety and health, and energy requirements are mandated by federal, state, and local regulations - failure to comply places program cost and schedule at risk and may result in civil or criminal enforcement against the FAA.

## What Must be Done

Determine what federal, state, and local requirements may apply to your investment program during investment analysis.

Ensure EOSHE requirements are reflected in program requirements and planning documents and in the contract statement of work and specification.

Refer to the [JRC Readiness Checklist](https://fast.faa.gov/Resources/_leaving.cfm?ref=FAST&uri=https://my.faa.gov/org/staffoffices/afn/acq_business/investment_process/jrc.html "JRC Readiness Checklist")  (FAA only) for additional guidance.

## Who Does It

Service teams and program offices plan, fund, and execute all EOSHE activities for their programs.

The Aviation Planning and Environmental Office assists with environmental, safety and health, and energy management policy issues .

ATO Technical Operations EOSH Services assists with technical issues regarding environmental, safety and health, and energy mandates.
