Statement of Work (SOW) samples listed below have been provided as peer recommendations only and may not represent current requirements. These documents have not been vetted by the program office or legal counsel. You are expected to modify or add SOW paragraphs to address specific requirements of your program. The SOW document can be opened and edited in Microsoft Word or other editors.
