### Service Analysis & Strategic Planning

Determine and plan for what capabilities must be in place now and in the future to meet agency mission responsibilities and the service needs of customers.

[AMS Policy Section 2.3](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf "Acquisition Practices Toolkit Service Analysis")

## [What Must be Done](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf#nameddest=policy2_3_1 "What Must Be Done")

Determine which service shortfalls/opportunities the FAA intends to resolve.

Incorporate highest priority shortfalls/opportunities into the Enterprise Architecture.

Plan for concept and requirements definition when investment action is needed.

## [Who Does It](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf#nameddest=policy2_3_3 "Who Does It")

Service organizations conduct service analysis - NextGen and IT offices assist.

Lines of business prioritize shortfalls and needs within their service domain.

Technical Review Board and Architecture Review Board work with lines of business to time-sequence new service shortfalls and needs into the Enterprise Architecture.

## [Who Approves](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf#nameddest=policy2_3_4_1 "Who Approves")

Joint Resources Council approves the FAA Enterprise Architecture.

FAA Enterprise Architecture Board approves EA products and amendments.

NextGen Lifecycle Integration Office and Director of the service organization with the need approve the preliminary shortfall analysis report.

Vice Presidents (ATO) or Directors (other LOBs) of the performing and operating service organizations and the FAA Enterprise Architecture Board chairperson approve the plan for CRD.

## [Key Outputs and Products](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf#nameddest=policy2_3_2_1 "Key Outputs and Products")

* [Shortfall Analysis Report](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/sartemplate.docx "Shortfall Analysis Report")
* Plan for Concept & Requirements Definition
* [Enterprise Architecture Products & Amendments](https://fast.faa.gov/Resources/_leaving.cfm?ref=FAST&uri=https://sep.faa.gov/file/get/3286 "Enterprise Architecture Products & Amendments ")  (FAA only)
* [Updates to the Enterprise Architecture](https://fast.faa.gov/Resources/_leaving.cfm?ref=FAST&uri=https://ksn2.faa.gov/faa/ea/SitePages/Home.aspx "Updates to the Enterprise Architecture")  (FAA only)

### NAS ConOps Change Process

Determine whether new service ideas and concepts should become part of the National Airspace System Concept of Operations

[AMS Policy Section 2.3](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf "AMS Policy Section 2.3")

## [What Must Be Done](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf#nameddest=policy2_3_1 "What Must Be Done")

Validate new concepts for inclusion in the NAS ConOps.

Develop business case for proposed operational capabilities.

Decompose approved operational sustainments and improvements into functional and performance requirements and investment increments.

## [Who Does It](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf#nameddest=policy2_3_3_2 "Who Does It")

Service organizations develop and validate new operational concepts with assistance of multiple NextGen and LOB organizations.

## [Who Approves](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf#nameddest=policy2_3_4_2 "NAS ConOps Change Development and Decomposition")

NextGen Management Board approves changes to the NAS ConOps and new operational capabilities. (Joint Resources Council concurs)

## [Key Outputs and Products](https://fast.faa.gov/docs/acquisitionManagementPolicy/AcquisitionManagementPolicy2.3.pdf#nameddest=policy2_3_2_2 "Key Outputs and Products")

* Updates to the NAS ConOps
* Updates to the NAS Requirements Document
* Updates to the NAS Operational Requirements Document
* Operational Capability Business Case
* Operational Capability Integration Plan
* Studies and reports validating new ideas and concepts
