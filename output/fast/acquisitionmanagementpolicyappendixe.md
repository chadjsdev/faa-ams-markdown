**Appendix E: External Authorities Revised 09/2020** 

This table highlights selected government-wide laws, regulations, executive orders, and other directives that affect acquisition programs. In some instances, FAA-unique implementation of these authorities is outlined in the Acquisition Management System. In most cases, however, implementation is through means other than the Acquisition Management System. This table is not all inclusive. Full text of the authorities may be viewed from the following websites: <u>http://www.archives.gov or http://uscode.house.gov</u> 

The appendix is divided into three parts as follows: 

Part I - Statutes arranged alphabetically by title 

Part II - Executive Orders arranged numerically 

Part III - Regulations/Standards arranged alphabetically by title
